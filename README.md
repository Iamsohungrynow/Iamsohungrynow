These days I work on neutral-atom quantum computing for my PhD, in David Wilkowski’s group at Nanyang Technological University / [Centre for Quantum Technologies](https://www.cqt.sg/). Before that I spent a year on Singapore’s first homegrown superconducting quantum computer, a 20-qubit processor, working on machine-learning methods to improve qubit readout, and wrote an [open introduction to superconducting qubits](https://martinalee.me/research/superconducting-qubits/).

I’ve been into crypto since 2019, way before I studied physics. I like zero-knowledge proofs, options and trading, so I tend to build around markets and privacy.

### Things I’ve built

- [**Anneal**](https://martinalee.me/projects/anneal/) <sub>Mar–May 2026</sub><br>An options DEX on Solana, built as a private OTC market: traders get competing quotes without revealing their budget.
- [**CrowdShift**](https://martinalee.me/projects/crowdshift/) <sub>Jul 2026 · solo</sub><br>A market that pays commuters to miss the crush: shift your trip off-peak and get paid once the shift is verified.
- [**Soliton**](https://martinalee.me/projects/soliton/) <sub>Jun 2026 · solo</sub><br>Verifiable memory for AI agents: each write gets a receipt on Sui, while the content is stored on Walrus.
- [**Agent Auction Protocol**](https://martinalee.me/projects/agent-auction-protocol/) <sub>Mar 2026</sub><br>Private auctions for autonomous AI agents: a computing-resources marketplace with verifiable settlement.
- [**Superconducting qubits from scratch**](https://martinalee.me/research/superconducting-qubits/) <sub>Jun 2026 · open course</sub><br>Twelve chapters and twelve QuTiP labs I compiled after teaching PH3410 Quantum Hardware.
